## Mon · 01_zeigarnik.mp4
Ever lie in bed replaying tasks you didn't finish? That's the Zeigarnik Effect: your brain keeps unfinished work "open."

The fix isn't doing everything. It's writing the next step down. A plan is enough to quiet the loop.

Save this for your evening shutdown routine.

Motivation isn't a plan. "If X happens, then I'll do Y" is.

Psychologists call these implementation intentions, and they're one of the best-supported tools for building habits.
